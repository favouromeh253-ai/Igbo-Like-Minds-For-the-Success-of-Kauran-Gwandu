National Integration and Unity

Promoting peaceful coexistence, inclusiveness, and stronger relationships among all communities in Kebbi State.

Building Unity Through Inclusive Governance

Governor Nasir Idris (Kauran Gwandu, Ezi-Enyi Ndigbo 1) has consistently promoted peaceful coexistence and unity among all ethnic groups in Kebbi State. His leadership emphasizes fairness, inclusion, and equal opportunity for all residents regardless of tribe or background.

Through continuous engagement with traditional leaders, youth groups, religious bodies, and community associations, his administration has strengthened trust and cooperation across diverse communities.

Inclusive Representation and Participation

The administration has ensured that people from different ethnic backgrounds are represented in governance through appointments, advisory roles, and participation in state development programs.

This inclusive approach has strengthened confidence in government and promoted peaceful coexistence among communities.

Support for the Igbo Community

The Igbo community in Kebbi State has contributed significantly to trade, commerce, and economic development. The administration maintains strong engagement with Igbo leaders and associations.

Support for community development projects, including renovation of cultural facilities such as the Igbo Hall, reflects the government's commitment to inclusion and respect for all groups.

Ezi-Enyi Ndigbo 1 – True Friend of the Igbo

Governor Nasir Idris was honored with the traditional title Ezi-Enyi Ndigbo 1, meaning “True Friend of the Igbo,” in recognition of his commitment to unity and peaceful coexistence.

This title reflects mutual respect and strong relationships built between his administration and the Igbo community in Kebbi State.
