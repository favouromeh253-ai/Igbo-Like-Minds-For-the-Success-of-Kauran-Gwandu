Women Empowerment

Strengthening Women as Key Drivers of Development

Governor Nasir Idris (Kauran Gwandu) recognizes the crucial role of women in sustainable growth. His administration has empowered women through skills development programs, small business grants, and cooperative support, targeting both urban and rural communities.

Initiatives focus on increasing economic independence, leadership participation, and access to quality education for women across Kebbi State. Rural towns such as Argungu, Gwandu, and Zuru have benefited from specialized outreach programs.
