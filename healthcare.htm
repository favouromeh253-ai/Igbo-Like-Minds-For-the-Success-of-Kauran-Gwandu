Healthcare Services

Improving Access to Quality Medical Care

Governor Nasir Idris (Kauran Gwandu) has enhanced healthcare infrastructure across Kebbi State. Hospitals and primary health centers have received upgrades, modern equipment, and improved staffing to ensure residents receive timely and quality care.

Programs prioritize maternal and child health, rural healthcare access, and preventive medicine. Communities in Birnin Kebbi, Zuru, and Argungu have benefited from these targeted interventions.
