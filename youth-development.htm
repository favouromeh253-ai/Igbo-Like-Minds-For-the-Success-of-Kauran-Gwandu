Youth Development

Equipping Young People for Employment and Leadership

The Kauran Gwandu administration has prioritized youth empowerment through vocational training, entrepreneurship programs, and employment schemes. These initiatives aim to reduce unemployment, foster self-reliance, and develop future leaders across Kebbi State.

Engagement programs also include sports, mentorship, and civic participation, giving young people opportunities to contribute meaningfully to society. Towns like Birnin Kebbi, Argungu, and Gwandu have active youth centers benefiting from these projects.
